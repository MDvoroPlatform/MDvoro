# MDvoro Israel Launch Legal Checklist — 2026

This document is an engineering/legal-readiness checklist, not legal advice. An Israeli lawyer should review the final business model, contracts, notices and content-rights workflow before public launch.

## 1. Privacy and databases

- Confirm the data-controller/operator legal entity and publish its legal name, address and privacy contact.
- Map every personal-data category: account data, learning activity, notes, subscriptions, security/audit data and support requests.
- Apply purpose limitation, access control, confidentiality, retention and deletion rules under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law.
- Determine whether the actual database must be registered or whether a notification duty applies under Amendment 13. The general registration duty was narrowed, but the underlying privacy/security duties remain.
- Document every external processor (hosting, database, email, payment, analytics) and the location of processing.
- Review cross-border transfers and the applicable Israeli transfer rules before launch.
- Maintain an information-security definition document, access-authority list, incident procedure, backup/recovery procedure and vendor controls appropriate to the database security level.
- Do not request or store patient-identifying medical information from students.

## 2. Israeli exam content / historical IMLE questions

- Do **not** assume that old IMLE questions are free to reproduce because they are old, were used in an examination, or can be found online.
- Every question must have a recorded rights basis before publication: original, licensed, permission, public-domain basis confirmed by counsel, or another documented lawful basis.
- Store source name/year/reference and verification evidence.
- If rights are unknown, keep the question unpublished.
- Keep the rights review separate from the medical-content review.
- Avoid implying affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship or official status with the Israeli Ministry of Health or licensing authorities.
- Do not copy logos, official branding, confidential exam material or restricted source documents without permission.

## 3. Copyright complaints

- Publish a real copyright contact and, if using a U.S. DMCA safe-harbor process, complete the required U.S. designated-agent registration and public notice process.
- Also maintain an Israel-first copyright complaint workflow under the Israeli Copyright Law.
- Preserve complaint records securely and restrict them to authorized staff.
- Have counsel define the response, counter-notice, repeat-infringer and restoration procedures applicable to MDvoro.

## 4. Consumer / subscription flow

- Publish the legal business identity, price, billing interval, renewal terms, taxes/fees and cancellation method clearly before payment.
- Provide the cancellation mechanism required for the actual Israeli distance transaction.
- Determine how the Consumer Protection Law applies to subscriptions, digital content and services supplied immediately after purchase.
- Keep transaction records and cancellation notices.
- Do not write a blanket “no refunds” rule without counsel review.

## 5. Accessibility

- Review the public and authenticated service against the Israeli internet-accessibility requirements.
- Publish an accessibility statement and real accessibility contact.
- Test keyboard navigation, focus states, labels, contrast, zoom/text resizing, RTL, screen readers and mobile touch targets.

## 6. Launch gating

The public launch should remain in **Under Construction** until the operator has completed:

1. Legal entity/contact details.
2. Final Hebrew Terms and Privacy Notice.
3. Israeli privacy/data-security assessment.
4. Vendor and cross-border data review.
5. Question-rights audit, especially historical IMLE material.
6. Accessibility review.
7. Consumer/payment/cancellation review.
8. Copyright/DMCA contact configuration where applicable.
9. Backup/restore test.
10. Production security and dependency audit.
