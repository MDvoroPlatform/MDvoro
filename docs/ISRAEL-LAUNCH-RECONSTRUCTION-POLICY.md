# MDvoro — Israel launch reconstruction policy

## Product label
Historical exam recollections are labeled **שחזור + year** (for example, **שחזור IMLE 2016**).

The label means the content is presented as a reconstruction/recollection of a historical examination experience. It is not a claim that the item is an official examination document, public-domain material, or owned by MDvoro.

## Legal-risk controls built into the product
- No per-question rights questionnaire is required for publication.
- No automated claim is made that an old IMLE question is public domain.
- MDvoro is described as independent and not affiliated with an exam authority unless a separate written relationship exists.
- A copyright/content complaint route is maintained.
- Content can be removed or disabled after a credible rights complaint.
- The site does not use “official IMLE” branding to imply endorsement.
- Terms and copyright pages reserve MDvoro's right to suspend or remove disputed material.

## Israel legal basis considered
The Israeli Copyright Law remains in force and was last amended in July 2026. Public availability of a work is not, by itself, a blanket permission to reproduce it. The Ministry of Justice describes the general rule as requiring permission for protected works, subject to statutory exceptions. See the National Legislation Database and Ministry of Justice guidance before launch.

Privacy, consumer, accessibility, and information-security obligations are handled separately in the site's legal pages and launch checklist.

This document is an engineering/compliance implementation note, not a legal opinion. A short Israeli lawyer review of the exact IMLE reconstruction workflow is recommended before commercial launch.
